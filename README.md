# 📊 Sales Data Analysis Using Superstore Dataset

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Synent Technologies Data Science Internship Program.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales data from a retail superstore and extract meaningful business insights related to revenue, profit, products, categories, customer segments, and regional performance.

The analysis helps identify trends, top-performing products, profitable categories, and areas requiring business improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze sales performance.
* Identify revenue trends over time.
* Find top-selling products.
* Evaluate profitability.
* Compare category and sub-category performance.
* Analyze regional and customer segment performance.
* Generate actionable business insights.

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

* Sales

## 🔄 Analysis Performed

### 1. Dataset Exploration

* Loaded the dataset.
* Examined dataset structure.
* Reviewed column information and statistics.

### 2. Data Quality Check

* Checked for missing values.
* Checked duplicate records.
* Validated dataset quality.

### 3. Data Preparation

* Converted date columns to datetime format.
* Extracted month and year information for trend analysis.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Analysis

* Analyzed monthly sales trends.
* Identified high and low revenue periods.

### 5. Top-Selling Products Analysis

* Identified best-performing products.
* Compared product sales performance.

### 6. Profit Analysis

* Evaluated overall profitability.
* Identified high-profit and low-profit products.

### 7. Category Analysis

* Compared sales and profit across categories.
* Evaluated category contribution.

### 8. Sub-Category Analysis

* Analyzed detailed product group performance.
* Identified strongest and weakest sub-categories.

### 9. Regional Performance Analysis

* Compared sales across regions.
* Evaluated regional profitability.

### 10. Customer Segment Analysis

* Compared customer segments.
* Analyzed contribution to sales and profit.

---

## 📈 Key Findings

* Revenue trends vary significantly across months.
* A small number of products contribute heavily to total sales.
* Certain categories generate higher profits than others.
* Some products generate sales but produce low profits.
* Regional performance differs considerably.
* Customer segments show different purchasing behaviors.
